Everyday Gut Wellness is an independent educational publication about everyday digestive routines: fiber variety, hydration, meal rhythm, movement, sleep, and knowing when a symptom deserves a doctor’s attention.

Why we exist

Most digestive advice online is either a supplement pitch or a wall of jargon. We write for ordinary adults who want calm, practical steps they can try this week, and clear signals for when self-care is not enough.

How we write

  • We base our guidance on published clinical nutrition guidelines, peer-reviewed gastroenterology research, and the Dietary Guidelines for Americans.
  • We make no miracle promises and publish no invented testimonials or case studies.
  • We separate everyday, benign irregularity from red-flag symptoms that need medical care.
  • We correct errors quickly. If you spot one, tell us at hello@everydaygutwellness.com.

How we are funded

We do not sell supplements, run sponsored posts, or join supplement affiliate programs. The site is supported by our own printable tools, the 14-Day Gut Clarity Workbook and the 30-Day Gut Routine Planner, sold through Gumroad.

What we are not

We are not a medical practice and cannot answer individual medical questions. Our materials are meant to help you notice patterns and have a clearer conversation with your own clinician.

Contact

Reader questions, corrections, and partnership inquiries: hello@everydaygutwellness.com. We usually reply within 2–3 business days.
