Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Everyday Gut Wellness ("we", "us") collects when you use everydaygutwellness.com, why we collect it, which services help us, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Information we collect

  • When you request the free 7-Day Gentle Gut Reset Guide: your first name (optional), your email address, whether you ticked the box to receive emails from us, and the date and page you signed up from.
  • When you buy a workbook or planner: checkout happens on Gumroad. Gumroad tells us your name, email address, and what you bought so we can support your order. We never see or store your card details.
  • When you browse the site: Google Analytics 4 collects aggregate usage data such as pages viewed, device type, approximate region, and how you arrived. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

We do not ask for, and do not want, health records, diagnoses, or symptom details. Please do not send us personal medical information.

How we use your information

  • To give you the free guide you asked for.
  • Only if you ticked the consent box: to email you the guide and a short series of practical digestive-routine notes, followed by occasional updates about new guides and our workbooks. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • To deliver and support digital products you buy.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase stores signup records (name, email, consent choice, signup date).
  • Systeme.io sends our emails to readers who gave consent, and handles unsubscribes.
  • Gumroad processes payments and delivers purchased files under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics measures site usage under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts the website.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. They process it only to provide their service to us.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. The site also saves a small note in your browser after you sign up, so you are not asked again. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time; the guide still works without them.

How long we keep information

We keep signup records while you remain subscribed or until you ask us to delete them. If you unsubscribe, we keep only what we need to make sure you are not emailed again. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for tax and accounting.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop emails: click “unsubscribe” at the bottom of any email.
  • See, correct, or delete your data: email hello@everydaygutwellness.com from the address you signed up with. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights under local law, and we honor them.

Children

This site is written for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@everydaygutwellness.com.
