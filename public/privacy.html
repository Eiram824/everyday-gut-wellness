Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This independent educational website requires your first name and email address to save a free-guide request and unlock the guide reader and PDF links. The request is processed by our site and stored in Supabase so we can manage your request and, when enabled, provide the requested email copy. Do not submit symptoms, diagnoses, or other sensitive health information through the form. Habit reflections and completed worksheet answers are not submitted or stored by the website.

After a successful request, this browser may store only an access preference ({"accessGranted":true}) so you can reopen the guide. It contains no name or email address. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser storage. This convenience preference is not secure download authentication: the PDF remains a public asset so existing email links continue to work.

Email and marketing consent

Requesting the guide is separate from signing up for marketing. Where email-copy delivery is enabled, the request address is processed by Systeme.io to provide the requested free guide. A guide request does not enroll you in a promotional email campaign. An optional educational series uses a separate sign-up and email-confirmation step. Its messages may include paid PDF offers, and you can unsubscribe using the link in those messages. We do not use your guide request to infer a health condition.

When bundle launch-alert registration is open, its separate form saves your first name, email address, the bundle-specific permission version, and request date in a private bundle-interest table in Supabase. Its checkbox is unchecked by default. This permission is separate from guide requests and broad marketing consent, so an existing guide requester can independently request a bundle alert. We do not automatically create a Systeme.io contact, apply an email tag, or enroll anyone in a campaign from this form.

This records interest only: the planned $39 bundle is not yet for sale and alert automation is not active yet. When registration is closed, the form does not save a signup or request an email. You can contact us to withdraw bundle-alert permission; we record a withdrawal date so another public submission cannot reactivate that permission. Repeated requests do not overwrite the original permission or withdrawal. Contact us separately to withdraw a guide email-copy request; the optional confirmed marketing series also has its own unsubscribe links.

Systeme.io may record delivery, email opens, and link clicks when it provides these emails. These records help us check delivery and working links. Please do not reply with symptoms, diagnoses, medical history, or completed health worksheets.

Analytics

The site loads Google Analytics to understand general site traffic. We do not intentionally send health symptoms or medical details to analytics.

Purchases

Paid digital products are sold and delivered through Gumroad. Gumroad processes payment and order delivery under its own terms and privacy practices; this site does not collect payment card numbers.

To avoid promoting a PDF to someone who has already bought from us, we may use purchase email addresses and order identifiers supplied by Gumroad to keep private buyer-exclusion records in Supabase and apply a buyer marker in Systeme.io. This may include the purchaser's or gift recipient's email address. Buyer classification does not enroll a person in marketing, change an unsubscribe preference, or restart a promotional series after a refund.

Requests and questions

For a product order, use the support link in your Gumroad receipt. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your signup details, or to withdraw guide-email or bundle-alert permission, email hello@everydaygutwellness.com or use the Contact page. You can separately unsubscribe from the confirmed marketing series through its email links. Withdrawing one permission does not sign you up for another type of email.
