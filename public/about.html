About Everyday Gut Wellness

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Everyday Gut Wellness is an independent educational publication about everyday digestive routines: fiber variety, hydration, meal rhythm, movement, sleep, and knowing when a symptom deserves a doctor’s attention.

Why we exist

Most digestive advice online is either a supplement pitch or a wall of jargon. We write for ordinary adults who want calm, practical steps they can try this week, and clear signals for when self-care is not enough.

Who runs this site

Everyday Gut Wellness is founded and edited by Mark Laurence E. Medina, based in Metro Manila, Philippines. Mark researches and writes each guide and keeps it up to date.

Why this site exists. For years, Mark often felt unwell because of gut problems. Building simple, steady daily habits around food, fluids, movement and routine made a real difference for him. He started this site to share what he learned, in plain language, with people going through the same thing. His belief is simple: when you start to respect your health, it gives back to you for years to come.

Mark is not a doctor, dietitian, or other licensed health professional, and the guides are not individually reviewed by a clinician. That is why every guide is built on public health guidance from organisations such as the NHS and the US National Institutes of Health, and on published research, which is listed at the end of each article so you can check it yourself.

How we write

  • We base our guidance on published clinical nutrition guidelines, peer-reviewed gastroenterology research, and the Dietary Guidelines for Americans.
  • We make no miracle promises and publish no invented testimonials or case studies.
  • We separate everyday, benign irregularity from red-flag symptoms that need medical care.
  • We correct errors quickly. If you spot one, tell us at hello@everydaygutwellness.com.

How we are funded

We do not sell supplements, run sponsored posts, or join supplement affiliate programs. The site is supported by our own printable tools, the 14-Day Gut Clarity Workbook and the 30-Day Gut Routine Planner, sold through Gumroad.

We also run a small online shop of drug-free comfort tools, such as a heat belt and a castor oil pack kit. Our belly comfort guides may mention these products, and when they do, we say so clearly. What we sell never changes what we write about the evidence: if something is not proven, we tell you.

What we are not

We are not a medical practice and cannot answer individual medical questions. Our materials are meant to help you notice patterns and have a clearer conversation with your own clinician.

Contact

Reader questions, corrections, and partnership inquiries: hello@everydaygutwellness.com. We usually reply within 2–3 business days.
