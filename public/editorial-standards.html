Editorial & Research Standards

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Everyday Gut Wellness is an independent educational project. Its pages and workbooks have not been independently reviewed by a clinician. Content is intended for general education and self-reflection, not diagnosis or treatment. References are included so readers can inspect underlying guidance and evidence themselves.

Sources relevant to the digestive habits guide

  1. NIDDK: Symptoms & Causes of Constipation — warning signs and when to seek prompt medical advice.
  2. NIDDK: Treatment for Constipation — general bathroom-routine and comfortable-positioning guidance.
  3. MedlinePlus: Fatigue — assessment of persistent or unexplained fatigue.
  4. NIDDK: Eating, Diet, & Nutrition for Constipation — general guidance on adult fiber ranges, gradual increases, and fluids.
  5. Van der Schoot A, et al. Fiber supplementation and chronic constipation in adults: updated systematic review and meta-analysis (2022) — trial evidence with variation by fiber type, treatment length, and tolerability.
  6. Mitsuhashi S, et al. Characterizing Normal Bowel Frequency and Consistency in a Representative Sample of Adults in the United States (NHANES) (2018) — population description, not an individual diagnostic standard.

How to interpret this page

Study findings do not guarantee a result for an individual. We distinguish general population guidance from personal care, avoid presenting commercial products as treatment, and direct readers to healthcare professionals for persistent, severe, or worsening symptoms.

Medical notice: This site provides general education, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
